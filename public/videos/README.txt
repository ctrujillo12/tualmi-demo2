How to get them: in the TikTok app, open your own video > Share > Save video,
or export the original clips from your editing app. Vertical 9:16, MP4 (H.264),
under ~10 MB each keeps the page fast.

WHERE THE LINKS GO: paste each TikTok's URL into the TIKTOKS list at the top
of src/app/page.tsx — the `url` field on each entry. Clicking a video on the
site opens that TikTok.
